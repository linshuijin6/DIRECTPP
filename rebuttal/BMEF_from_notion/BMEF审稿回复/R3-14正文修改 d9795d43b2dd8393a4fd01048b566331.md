## 修改 1：弱化直接临床可用性

**位置：** Discussion，第 175 行（第三段）；与 R2-20 共享位置。

**Original**

```
Beyond its technical merits, the primary significance of DIReCT$++$ lies in its potential to democratize access to critical biomarker profiling, thereby addressing a major bottleneck in the early and precise management of dementia. By generating accurate proxies for both amyloid and metabolic PET from a single routine MRI scan, our framework effectively translates the gold-standard biological definition of AD into a scalable tool for clinical practice. The clinical viability of this approach is strongly evidenced by our results on personalized MCI diagnosis and stratification (Fig.~\ref{fig:5}B, C), where the combination of MRI and synthetic multi-tracer PET achieved performance comparable to the resource-intensive ideal of MRI with real PET, significantly outperforming models using MRI or real PET alone.
```

```
Beyond its technical merits, the primary significance of DIReCT$++$ lies in its potential to improve access to biomarker-related imaging information, thereby addressing a major challenge in the early assessment of dementia. By generating synthetic estimates of both amyloid and metabolic PET from a single routine MRI scan, our framework offers a scalable approach for multimodal imaging research. The potential clinical relevance of this approach is supported by our results on personalized MCI diagnosis and stratification (Fig.~\ref{fig:5}B, C), where the combination of MRI and synthetic multi-tracer PET achieved performance comparable to MRI with real PET in the evaluated cohort.
```

**修改理由：** 沿用 R2-20 对前两句的既定调整，取消“democratize access”“gold-standard biological definition 转化为临床工具”“clinical viability strongly evidenced”和未经直接统计检验支持的 “significantly”。仍保留实际开展的 MCI 分类结果。

## 修改 2：删除直接筛查、治疗决策与纵向监测的过度承诺

**位置：** Discussion，第 177 行（第四段）；与 R2-20 共享位置。

**Original**

```
This capability has profound implications. It could enable widespread screening of individuals at the preclinical or prodromal stages in primary care settings, identifying those with underlying AD pathology who are most likely to benefit from disease-modifying therapies. Furthermore, the ability to serially generate synthetic PET biomarkers from longitudinal MRI without additional radiation exposure opens new avenues for cost-effective monitoring of disease progression and treatment response. **Revised**
```

**修改理由：** 移除未经验证的基层广泛筛查、抗淀粉样蛋白治疗候选者筛选及已经具备标准临床工作流程适用性的表述。保留长期监测作为待验证方向，而非已证实的应用。R2-20 的先前拟稿仍含 “widespread screening”，本处以 R3-14 的审稿回复立场纠正这一冲突。

## 修改 3：限定相对于既有方法的创新与临床结论

**位置：** Discussion，第 179 行（第五段）。

**Original**

```
When contextualized within the field of medical image synthesis, the advancements of DIReCT$++$ represent a qualitative leap beyond prior approaches. While existing generative adversarial networks and diffusion models have demonstrated the feasibility of MRI-to-PET translation, they have primarily focused on achieving visual realism. DIReCT$++$ moves beyond this by achieving precise generation of subject-specific biomarkers that are clinically actionable. This is a direct consequence of its VLM-modulated architecture, which incorporates domain knowledge as a critical regularizer, a feature largely absent in preceding purely, data-driven approaches. The core principle of using a domain-adapted foundation model to guide an efficient generative process establishes a new paradigm that is extendible well beyond the synthesis of $^{18}$F-AV-45 and $^{18}$F-FDG PET. By prioritizing clinically grounded precision over mere voxel-level fidelity, our work points the way toward a new generation of AI models that are truly co-designed with clinical application in mind.
```

**Revised**

```
When contextualized within the field of medical image synthesis, DIReCT$++$ extends prior approaches by incorporating domain-informed guidance into multi-tracer PET synthesis. Existing generative adversarial networks and diffusion models have demonstrated the feasibility of MRI-to-PET translation. DIReCT$++$ further evaluates disease-related regional uptake patterns and downstream classification performance. Its VLM-modulated architecture incorporates domain knowledge to guide the generative process. This approach may be applicable beyond the synthesis of $^{18}$F-AV-45 and $^{18}$F-FDG PET, although broader validation is required.
```

**修改理由：** 删去 “qualitative leap”“clinically actionable”“direct consequence”“new paradigm”等无法由当前实验单独证明的强结论；保留多示踪剂合成、领域引导、区域摄取分析及分类评价这些可核查的实际贡献。不在此处新增与 R3-8/9 有关的机制消融结论。

## 修改 4：在已有 Limitations 中补充 2024 AD 标准及 p-tau217

**位置：** Discussion，第 186 行（Limitations 段）。仅在现有血液生物标志物表述后插入两句，后半段原文不动。

**Original**

```
Several limitations of this study present clear avenues for future work. First, the current text guidance relies on fundamental patient information and imaging knowledge. Incorporating additional clinically accessible data, such as blood biomarkers~\cite{hansson2023blood}, could further enhance subject-level precision, necessitating the development of targeted cross-modal pretraining or prompt-learning strategies.
```

**Revised**

```
Several limitations of this study present clear avenues for future work. First, the current text guidance relies on fundamental patient information and imaging knowledge. Incorporating additional clinically accessible data, such as blood biomarkers~\cite{hansson2023blood}, could further enhance subject-level precision, necessitating the development of targeted cross-modal pretraining or prompt-learning strategies. The 2024 revised Alzheimer's Association criteria emphasize the biological diagnosis of AD using validated core biomarkers, including amyloid PET and sufficiently accurate blood-based assays such as plasma p-tau217~\cite{jack2024revised}. Synthetic PET has not been validated as a substitute for molecular confirmation, and its added value relative to these established biomarker assessments remains to be determined.
```

**修改理由：** 以两句补充英文 Response 已承诺的 2024 生物学诊断框架、准确度合格的血浆 p-tau217 以及本研究尚未建立独立病理确证效度的局限；不改变已写好的其余 Limitations。
