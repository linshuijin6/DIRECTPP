## 一、Materials and Data Preprocessing — 临床数据处理说明

**位置：** `manuscript/BMEF_main.tex`，第 297 行附近，ADNI 临床特征段落。

**Original**

```
A consistent gradient in disease severity was observed across groups. Mean Mini-Mental State Examination (MMSE) scores declined progressively from 29.40 in CN to 20.84 in AD ($p < 0.001$, trend across groups). Correspondingly, functional and neuropsychiatric burden increased with diagnostic severity, as evidenced by higher scores on the Functional Activities Questionnaire (FAQ), Geriatric Depression Scale (GDSCALE), and Neuropsychiatric Inventory–Questionnaire (NPI-Q) in AD relative to CN and MCI. Significant intergroup differences were also observed in demographic variables, including age and sex distribution (see Table~\ref{tab:supp_adni}).
```

**Revised**

```
In the prepared clinical data, mean Mini-Mental State Examination (MMSE) scores decreased from 29.40 in CN to 20.84 in AD. Other clinical assessments, including the Functional Activities Questionnaire (FAQ), Geriatric Depression Scale (GDSCALE), and Neuropsychiatric Inventory–Questionnaire (NPI-Q), also varied across diagnostic groups. To provide complete clinical conditioning inputs, some missing clinical assessment scores were simulated based on diagnostic-group information. Consequently, the reported clinical score distributions may reflect both observed and simulated values and should not be interpreted as independent evidence of between-group clinical differences. Demographic characteristics, including age and sex distributions, are summarized in Table~\ref{tab:supp_adni}.
```

**修改理由：** 保留原稿已经报告的评分趋势，明确诊断组条件模拟补全；删除基于这类临床评分的显著性推断，避免把模拟后的组间差异当作独立临床发现。

## 三、Discussion — Limitations

**位置：** `manuscript/BMEF_main.tex`，第 186 行附近。

**Original**

```
Several limitations of this study present clear avenues for future work. First, the current text guidance relies on fundamental patient information and imaging knowledge. Incorporating additional clinically accessible data, such as blood biomarkers~\cite{hansson2023blood}, could further enhance subject-level precision, necessitating the development of targeted cross-modal pretraining or prompt-learning strategies. Second, while DIReCT$++$ synthesizes $^{18}$F-AV-45 and $^{18}$F-FDG, extending the framework to include tau-PET imaging would provide a more comprehensive biomarker profile for sensitive screening at the preclinical stage. Third, to maximize clinical utility, future iterations could integrate downstream task guidance (e.g., diagnosis or prognosis labels) directly into the generative model, enabling controllable synthesis~\cite{wang2025flexibly,chang2025controllable} tailored for specific clinical decisions. Finally, the evaluation was conducted on well-characterized but limited research cohorts (ADNI, OASIS). Prospective validation on more diverse, real-world clinical populations (including data from thick-slice or low-field MRI~\cite{sorby2024portable}) is essential to fully establish the generalizability and robustness of the framework.
```

**Revised**

```
Several limitations of this study present clear avenues for future work. First, the current text guidance relies on fundamental patient information and imaging knowledge. The use of diagnostic-group-conditioned simulation for some missing clinical scores may introduce diagnosis-related information into the conditioning inputs, which should be considered when interpreting downstream classification performance. Incorporating additional clinically accessible data, such as blood biomarkers~\cite{hansson2023blood}, could further enhance subject-level precision, necessitating the development of targeted cross-modal pretraining or prompt-learning strategies. Second, while DIReCT$++$ synthesizes $^{18}$F-AV-45 and $^{18}$F-FDG, extending the framework to include tau-PET imaging would provide a more comprehensive biomarker profile for sensitive screening at the preclinical stage. Third, to maximize clinical utility, future iterations could integrate downstream task guidance (e.g., diagnosis or prognosis labels) directly into the generative model, enabling controllable synthesis~\cite{wang2025flexibly,chang2025controllable} tailored for specific clinical decisions. Finally, the evaluation was conducted on well-characterized but limited research cohorts (ADNI, OASIS). Prospective validation on more diverse, real-world clinical populations (including data from thick-slice or low-field MRI~\cite{sorby2024portable}) is essential to fully establish the generalizability and robustness of the framework.
```

**修改理由：** 仅增加一句，明确诊断组条件模拟可能影响下游分类结果的解释，不在论文正文中重复审稿回复的详细辩解，也不承诺新实验。
