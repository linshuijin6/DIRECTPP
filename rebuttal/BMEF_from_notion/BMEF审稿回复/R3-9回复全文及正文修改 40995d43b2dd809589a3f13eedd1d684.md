# R3-9回复全文及正文修改

感谢审稿人对消融实验完整性提出的建议。我们认同，进一步区分文本引导、临床条件编码、示踪剂适配及采样策略的贡献，有助于更全面地评价 DIReCT++ 各组成部分的作用。

针对这一意见，我们重新梳理了已有对比实验，并针对关键模块开展了补充消融分析。考虑到不同模块在模型中的功能及其验证方式有所区别，我们将训练级消融与推理阶段的条件干预相结合，以提供更有针对性的实验依据。

首先，对于文本条件的贡献，原稿中已有的 3D RF 基线不采用 BiomedCLIP 增强的文本引导，可作为无文本条件的相关对照。我们也注意到，该基线与 DIReCT++ 存在其他实现差异，因此不能将其性能差异完全归因于文本引导。为进一步分析这一因素，我们将使用训练完成的 Full 模型，在固定网络参数的情况下移除文本条件进行推理，评价模型对文本引导的依赖程度。

对于临床条件的独立贡献，我们已开展 Full 与 Only-tracer 的从头训练对照，以评估在保留示踪剂条件的情况下，患者临床信息对 PET 合成性能的影响。此外，我们也已补充 BiomedCLIP 文本编码器与结构化临床变量编码器的训练对照，以进一步评价 VLM 文本表示相对于常规数值特征编码的作用。

关于 Clinical prompt only，需要说明的是，DIReCT++ 采用共享的多示踪剂条件生成框架，其中 tracer token 不仅提供示踪剂相关的领域信息，还负责指定目标 PET 模态。若完全移除该 token，仅保留患者临床信息，模型将无法区分 AV45-PET 与 FDG-PET 的生成目标。因此，这一配置不能在保持现有模型架构和任务定义的情况下构成完整的双示踪剂生成对照。

对于单步蒸馏与多步 ODE 采样，我们优先利用已有的模型 checkpoint，比较常规 Rectified Flow 多步推理与蒸馏后单步推理的生成质量和计算效率，以分析不同生成策略之间的权衡。

对于联合与独立示踪剂生成，原稿已包含 DIReCT Single-tracer 对照，用于评价单示踪剂方法与 DIReCT++ 共享多示踪剂框架的整体性能差异。我们将在修订稿中进一步明确这一比较的范围，避免将两种方法之间的全部差异归因于联合训练本身。

此外，针对 Frozen 与 Adapted tracer embedding 的比较，我们将结合示踪剂表示适配机制的相关分析进一步讨论其独立贡献，避免在缺乏对应实验支持的情况下过度解释适配模块的作用。

本研究的完整训练需要较高的计算开销，原稿也已报告单次训练约需 138 GPU-hours（6 days）。在有限的修订周期内（21days），我们优先将重新训练资源投入临床条件贡献和 VLM 编码方式这两项关键问题，同时利用已有对照和推理干预分析其他因素。我们将在修订稿中明确区分不同实验的验证目的及证据范围，以更准确地展示各模块对 PET 合成性能的贡献。

## English Response — R3-9

*归档说明：以下英文按已确认的 R3-9 中文回复方向整理。历史英文定稿虽已确认，但未能逐字恢复；本段不冒充历史逐字稿。*

We thank the reviewer for the constructive suggestion to strengthen the ablation study. We agree that distinguishing the contributions of text guidance, clinical conditioning, tracer representation adaptation, and sampling strategy is important for a more comprehensive assessment of DIReCT++.

We revisited the existing comparisons and supplemented the analyses of key conditioning components. Because the components serve different functions, we distinguish comparisons involving independently trained models from interventions applied only during inference, rather than treating them as interchangeable forms of ablation.

First, regarding text conditioning, the existing 3D rectified flow (3D RF) baseline does not use the BiomedCLIP-enhanced text guidance. However, it also differs from DIReCT++ in other implementation aspects; hence, the performance difference cannot be attributed exclusively to the text guidance module. To further examine sensitivity to text conditioning, we propose evaluating the trained Full model with the text condition removed at inference while keeping the network parameters fixed. Such an intervention assesses dependence on the conditioning input but is not equivalent to training a text-free model.

Second, to evaluate the independent contribution of patient-specific clinical information, we conducted a training-level comparison between the Full and Only-tracer configurations, which retains tracer guidance while excluding the patient clinical condition. We also added a training comparison between BiomedCLIP-based text encoding and an encoder for structured clinical variables to assess the contribution of the VLM representation relative to conventional numerical conditioning. These comparisons address the clinical-conditioning and encoder-design aspects of the requested ablations.

Regarding a clinical-prompt-only configuration, the tracer token in our shared multi-tracer framework not only carries tracer-related information but also specifies the target PET modality. Removing it entirely while retaining only the patient clinical information would leave the network without an explicit distinction between AV45 and FDG generation targets. Therefore, this configuration would not represent a well-defined dual-tracer comparison under the current architecture and task definition.

For one-step distillation versus multi-step ODE sampling, we prioritize comparisons using available model checkpoints to assess image quality and computational efficiency across the sampling strategies. For joint versus separate tracer generation, the original manuscript already includes a single-tracer DIReCT comparison. We will clarify that this evaluates the overall difference between separately trained single-tracer models and the shared multi-tracer framework, rather than isolating a synergistic benefit from joint training alone.

Finally, the contribution of frozen versus adapted tracer embeddings will be discussed together with the analysis of tracer representation adaptation. We will avoid attributing a performance gain solely to the adaptation module without an appropriately controlled comparison.

The original manuscript reports approximately 138 GPU-hours for a complete model training run. Given this computational cost, we have prioritized training-level comparisons for clinical conditioning and the representation of clinical variables, while using existing baselines and targeted inference analyses where appropriate. We will distinguish the purposes and evidential limitations of these experiments in the revised manuscript, without interpreting a baseline comparison as a controlled single-component ablation.
