Regional analysis used 98 SynthSeg ROIs defined on T1-weighted MRI. Representative regions shown in Fig.~\ref{fig:4}C,D were selected for disease-related comparisons. FDG regions included the bilateral hippocampus, isthmus cingulate cortex and supramarginal gyrus (atlas labels \texttt{Left-Hippocampus}, \texttt{Right-Hippocampus}, and the \texttt{ctx-lh-}/\texttt{ctx-rh-} labels with suffixes \texttt{isthmuscingulate} and \texttt{supramarginal})~\cite{mosconi2005reduced,he2015meta,strom2022cortical}. AV45 regions included bilateral precuneus and inferior, middle, superior and transverse temporal gyri (the corresponding \texttt{ctx-lh-}/\texttt{ctx-rh-} labels)~\cite{wong2010in}. Atlas-defined names are used rather than the ambiguous temporoparietal-junction label.
