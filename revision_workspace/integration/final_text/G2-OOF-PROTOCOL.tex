PET synthesis used subject-level two-fold cross-validation with fixed, approximately equal partitions (929 and 928 participants; split seed 42). In each fold, training and held-out participants were disjoint. Held-out predictions from the two folds were combined into an out-of-fold (OOF) dataset for generation analyses, with 1,598 valid FDG and 1,365 valid AV45 observations before the separately specified MRI/mask QC exclusion used in masked evaluation. Downstream classification used a separate task-specific stratified three-fold protocol, described below. The classification analysis retained the existing author-provided synthetic PET dataset under the intended two-fold OOF design; its image-to-checkpoint correspondence to the reconstructed Full generation experiments could not be independently established. The classification results are therefore analyzed separately from the reconstructed generator comparisons.
