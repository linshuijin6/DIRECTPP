The historical OASIS-3 external cohort comprised 419 PUP-processed AV45 participants (334 CN, 85 AD); the 117 FDG participants without the corresponding processing were not included. The age distributions differed between diagnostic groups (Table~\ref{tab:supp_oasis}). Reported clinical summaries do not establish which complete prompt fields were used in every external run or how unavailable fields were handled.
