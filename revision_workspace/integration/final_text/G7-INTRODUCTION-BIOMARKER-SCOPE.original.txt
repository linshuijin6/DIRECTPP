This challenge is underscored by the modern biological definition of AD, which is based on in vivo evidence of core pathologies, specifically amyloid-$\beta$ (A$\beta$) deposition (e.g., on $^{18}$F-AV45 PET) and neuronal injury (e.g., $^{18}$F-FDG PET hypometabolism), with structural MRI quantifying associated atrophy~\cite{ossenkoppele2022amyloid,yan2021sex,haller2023neuroimaging}.
