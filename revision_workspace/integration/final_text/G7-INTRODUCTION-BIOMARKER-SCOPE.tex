AD evaluation uses complementary molecular, functional and structural measurements: amyloid-$\beta$ (A$\beta$) deposition can be assessed with $^{18}$F-AV45 PET, glucose-metabolic dysfunction with $^{18}$F-FDG PET, and associated atrophy with structural MRI~\cite{ossenkoppele2022amyloid,yan2021sex,haller2023neuroimaging}. These modalities are not interchangeable; in particular, FDG hypometabolism and MRI atrophy do not by themselves establish the biological diagnosis of AD~\cite{jack2024revised}.
