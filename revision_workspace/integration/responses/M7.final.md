We thank the reviewer for pointing out the inconsistent nomenclature and citations for the brain parcellation method across the manuscript. We acknowledge that the use of “mri_synthseg (FreeSurfer v7.4.1)” and “SynthSeg” in different sections, together with inconsistent references, may have caused confusion.

To address this issue, we now consistently refer to the ROI parcellation method as **SynthSeg** and standardize its citation throughout the manuscript. We will also clearly distinguish the FreeSurfer-based skull-stripping step from SynthSeg-based brain parcellation and clarify that the ROI masks derived from T1-weighted MRI were used for regional PET analysis. These revisions will ensure a consistent and accurate description of the preprocessing and ROI analysis procedures.
