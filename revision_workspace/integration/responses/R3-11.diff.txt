 Finally, regarding composite SUVR scatter plots and amyloid-positivity analysis, we acknowledge the clinical relevance of these assessments. However, the PET intensities in the present study underwent per-image min–max normalization, and the resulting regional uptake measurements are not clinically calibrated SUVR or Centiloid values. Applying conventional clinical SUVR thresholds or deriving amyloid-positivity classifications directly from these normalized intensities would therefore risk producing quantitatively unsupported conclusions. We have clarified this limitation and identify standardized PET quantification and amyloid-status agreement as directions for future investigation.
