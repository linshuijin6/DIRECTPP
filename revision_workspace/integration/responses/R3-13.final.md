We thank the reviewer for the constructive comments regarding the scope of external validation and the clinical data used in OASIS-3.

We agree that our external validation on OASIS-3 was limited to AV45-PET synthesis in cognitively normal (CN) and Alzheimer’s disease (AD) participants. These results therefore do not establish cross-dataset generalizability for FDG-PET synthesis or MCI-related tasks. As also addressed in our response to Reviewer 2, Comment 7, we now clarify these limitations and appropriately qualify the corresponding generalizability claims in the revised manuscript.

The retained external cohort comprised PUP-processed AV45-PET data from CN and AD participants, with no MCI participants. Complete run-specific records of the clinical prompt fields and missing-field handling were not retained, limiting reproducibility of that historical evaluation. We also acknowledge the age difference between the CN and AD groups, which should be considered when interpreting the external validation results.

We appreciate the reviewer’s suggestion and will ensure that our conclusions accurately reflect the scope and limitations of the available external validation evidence.
